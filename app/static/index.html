What this can and can't tell you

        
    
          
  • A highlight means check this character. It does not mean the character is wrong.
    • 
          
  • A character with no highlight is not proven right.
    • 
          
  • Where no ink is left on the paper, nothing can be recovered. Text shown there is a guess.
    • 
          
  • Built for printed English thermal receipts, not handwriting or other documents.
